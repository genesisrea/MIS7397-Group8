This workflow is designed to improve how quickly and consistently LTS data issues are detected, reviewed, and acted on. The KPIs below measure the impact of the automated Power BI → Power Automate → AI escalation process compared to traditional manual review. 
- Time‑to‑Detection (TTD)- how long it takes for an OOC or trend violation to be identified.  
    - Baseline: Manual review can delay detection.
    - With workflow: Power BI flags issues immediately after refresh 
- Time‑to‑Notification (TTN) - how long it takes for personnel to be notified after detection.
    - Baseline: Manual emails or missed reviews can delay notification.
    - With workflow: Power Automate sends notifications automatically.
 -Time-to-Review (TTR) - Time required for a technician to gather relevant information and reach an initial assessment of a flagged SPC Event.
    -Baseline: Technician manually reviews: Previous Runs, Daily Environmental Logs, Carrier Gas Pressures, Instrument History
    -With workflow: Technician receives event summary, historical trend summary, environmental context, possible causes, and suggested checks. 


The value of AI is reducing the time and effort required for laboratory personnel to gather contextual information and interpret flagged events. Rather than replacing human judgment, AI compiles relevant evidence, summarizes the event, identifies notable patterns, and presents possible explanations for review by qualified staff.
This project evaluates whether AI-generated event summaries provide a measurable operational benefit compared to a traditional review process.

Hypothesis: AI-generated event summaries will reduce the time required for a technician to review an SPC exception while maintaining acceptable review quality and consistency.
