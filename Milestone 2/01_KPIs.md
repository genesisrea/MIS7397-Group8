This workflow is designed to improve how quickly and consistently LTS data issues are detected, reviewed, and acted on. The KPIs below measure the impact of the automated Power BI → Power Automate → AI escalation process compared to traditional manual review. 
- Time‑to‑Detection (TTD)- how long it takes for an OOC or trend violation to be identified.  
    - Baseline: Manual review can delay detection.
    - With workflow: Power BI flags issues immediately after refresh 
- Time‑to‑Notification (TTN) - how long it takes for personnel to be notified after detection.
    - Baseline: Manual emails or missed reviews can delay notification.
    - With workflow: Power Automate sends notifications automatically.
